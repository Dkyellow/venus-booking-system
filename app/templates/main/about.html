About Us

Trusted Healthcare Since 2014

Founded in 2014 by Dr. Knowledge Tsungu, Venus Medical & Dental Centre has grown from a single dental practice into a multidisciplinary healthcare provider.

Our Story

A Multidisciplinary Practice
Under One Roof

Today, we offer medical, dental, mental health, diagnostic, preventative, and specialist services in Mount Pleasant, Harare. This comprehensive approach means you and your family can receive whole-person care in one trusting environment.

Our team of experienced professionals is committed to delivering compassionate, patient-centred care. We combine modern technology with personalised attention to ensure every patient receives the best possible outcome.
