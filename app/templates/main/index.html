About Us

Trusted Healthcare
Since 2014

Founded in 2014 by Dr. Knowledge Tsungu, Venus Medical & Dental Centre has grown from a single dental practice into a multidisciplinary healthcare provider. Today, we offer medical, dental, mental health, diagnostic, preventative, and specialist services under one roof in Mount Pleasant, Harare.

Our team of experienced professionals is committed to delivering compassionate, patient-centred care. We combine modern technology with personalised attention to ensure every patient receives the best possible outcome.
